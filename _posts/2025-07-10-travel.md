# Arrival

![Milan and Como](/assets/images/milan-como.png)

Mandello Del Lario, a small town on the East shore of Lake Como is a 1-hour train ride or 90-minute car ride from Milan

We recommnend this travel itinerary:
1. Fly into Milan's Malpensa airport, arriving in the morning June 5th.
2. Train to Mandelo de Lario, with a transfer at Milan Central station ("Milano Centrale")
3. Villa Confalonieri is a short 4-minute walk from the train station, about 2 city blocks south on the street "Via Armando Diaz"

You can book train tickets on [ItaliaRail.com](https://shop.italiarail.com/results?departureStationId=830025635&arrivalStationId=830001415&departureDate=2026-06-05&adults=2&children=0) (This link will show Malpensa to Mandello de Lario for June 5th)

You can also buy train tickets at the sation kiosks, but tickets bought at these kiosks need to be stamped/validated by the scanners before you board the train.

For taxi service around Lake Como, including to/from Malpensa airport, you can use [Lake Como Taxi](https://www.lakecomotaxi.com/) - text with Whatsapp. 

We recommend downloading the Trenitalia app for [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trenitalia/id331050847) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lynxspa.prontotreno&hl=en-US) - you can search and purchase tickets in the app, and do not need to validate these tickets at the train station.
