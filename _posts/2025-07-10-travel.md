# Arrival

Mandello del Lario, a small town on the eastern shore of Lake Como, is a 1-hour train ride from Milan’s Central Train Station (Milano Centrale) or a 2-hour ride from Milan’s Malpensa Airport.

Train tickets are 15-25€ and can be purchased on [Trenitalia](https://www.lefrecce.it) or [ItaliaRail](https://www.italiarail.com) 

We recommend downloading the Trenitalia app for [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/trenitalia/id331050847) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lynxspa.prontotreno&hl=en-US) - you can search and purchase tickets in the app, and do not need to validate these tickets at the train station.
